# Fall World

### ▶ **https://sjgant80-hub.github.io/fallworld/** · [how it all fits together](https://sjgant80-hub.github.io/fallworld/ecosystem.html)

**Hatch a didy. Grow it. Collect the whole estate as cards. Every rank is earned from what actually ran.**

Fall World is the whole estate as one game you install. Your didy is your character: an egg that
already works with no model and no key, which hatches in your browser tab and grows, one measured
stage at a time, on your own machine. Every public build in the estate is a card whose rarity is
computed from what GitHub's own runners did to it. The creatures breed into ones that measurably
work better; in battle a bigger model does not win; and you meet other people browser to browser.

## The living world

Simon, 2026-09-30: *"where are the cards … the didys hatch into webllm first then 7b 14b 70 200b …
weaving everything we have into one whole glorious ecosystem"* and *"the current fall world is old
needs fully improving with all this"*. Upgraded in place, woven from builds that already run:

| Move | What it is | Made real by |
|---|---|---|
| **Hatch** | an egg that works with no model; a hatchling in your tab | fall-os, didy, WebLLM via fall-kit |
| **Grow** | egg → ~1B → … → ~100–200B, only when a measured bar says so | fallforgemint's sizer ladder + the sealed trial of rules |
| **Collect** | every public build as a card; rarity from evidence, art from genes | world.json, `deck.mjs`, fallkard-forge |
| **Breed** | creatures selected on real work; a child only from a sovereign coupling | kard-evolve, offspring |
| **Battle** | one body each, so size does not win; specials must pass their gate | didy-arena, fallkard |
| **Meet** | browser to browser, no server in the middle | meshos, the Armoury |
| **Prove** | every rank from a check that ran | witness, proof-of-play, earned |
| **Multiply** | fork a didy for your guild; estates hatch estates | guild-didy, generative-estate |

- **The Deck** (press K): every card, rarest first, with the rarity table counted from evidence and the

- **Grow** (press G): the stages, what each asks of a machine, the sealed trial and its result, and a
  button that hatches the same size of model in your own tab and takes the same trial there.
- **[How it all fits together](https://sjgant80-hub.github.io/fallworld/ecosystem.html)**: the whole

### The trial of rules

A didy grows to the **smallest** stage that clears the job's bar, and shrinks when a smaller one will
do. The trial that decides it was sealed before any model was asked (`data/grow-prereg.json`,
`scripts/grow-trial.mjs`): 60 real cards, the world's own rarity rule, one word back, graded by the
rule itself; bar 57 of 60. Every model ran on one machine through Ollama. CI re-grades every recorded
reply on each push, and the page re-grades them again in your browser.

### Three kinds of thing, never blended

Your builds (Simon Gant), foundations the estate stands on (Thomas's original builds among them), and
wrappers around services nobody here owns. They are kept apart because the dishonesty runs both ways:
showing someone else's work as yours takes credit you have not earned, and hiding the work your
estate was built on erases the person who did it. The attribution is generated onto every card.

## Why it reads like a game

Because a thousand repositories are illegible to everyone, including the person who built them, and
*cards, rarity, levels, evolution* is vocabulary every human already holds. Each game word maps onto
machinery that already exists and is already gated:

| Game word | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| your character | an agent bounded by a capability grant and a budget |
| evolution | the smallest model size that clears a measured bar |
| rarity | how much evidence a build carries, computed from its CI |
| breeding | selection on real work, or two lines joined under the birth law |
| item level | whether it passed a gate — unproven gear cannot be equipped |
| the raid | trustless cross-verification with peers who do not trust you |

## Rarity is proof-strength, not scarcity

In most games an item is gold because of a dice roll. Here every rarity is **computed from evidence**,
first match wins, and each card carries the reason it got its colour:

| | Rarity | Earned by |
|---|---|---|
| 🟥 | **Unidentified** | it exists and nothing anywhere says what it does |
| ⬜ | **Normal** | described, but nothing live to open |
| 🟦 | **Magic** | live, described, open it right now |
| 🟨 | **Rare** | live and ships the full api/mcp/sdk set |
| 🟧 | **Unique** | a machine tried to break it and it held |
| 🟩 | **Set** | gated *and* ships the full set — it combos |

## Generated, never typed

Every number on the pages comes from the estate index, GitHub's own record of what ran, the vendored
ladder and creatures, and the sealed trial:

```bash
node scripts/scan-ci.mjs && node scripts/scan-tiers.mjs   # what GitHub's runners did
node scripts/build-world.mjs                               # the cards
node scripts/sync-sources.mjs                              # the ladder and the creatures, pinned
node scripts/build-client.mjs && node scripts/build-ecosystem.mjs
```

CI rebuilds both pages, `llms.txt` and the sitemap, and fails if anything committed differs.
