Where you fight

The game part. Start at the Duel — it is the one that teaches you the rest.

The Duel Your first proper match. A board, a turn, a hand of cards. Start here. start here The Campaign Nine chapters and thirty bosses to work through on your own. The Arena You watch AI players fight each other and get better at it. The Coliseum Play real people. Nobody runs a server — the match signs itself. The Guild Hall Join a guild, run a tournament, chase the banners.

Where you build

Making your character better. This is the bit people lose evenings to.

The Loom Build your deck. Thirty cards, and you can see whether it is any good. The Cube Put runes in your cards, craft new ones, break the ones you do not want. The Brain Give a card a real mind. It can use a proper AI model, on your machine. The Forge Put a working build inside a picture. Send the picture; they get the build.

Where the work gets checked

The bit nobody else has. Nothing here takes your word for it.

The Clinic Reads back what actually happened in your matches and tells you what is really going on. The Proving Ground You cannot say you can do a thing. You show it, and it gets checked. The Gate The wall on the edge of the world. Costs you a moment, costs a scraper months. The Watchtower Look at the outside world, and judge what it did rather than what it said.

Where you earn

The money side. Your stuff is yours and it can pay you.

The Herd Your descendants wake up and go to work, and the earnings come back up to you. The Bloodline Own a share of a line of cards. When they do well, so do you. $KONO Mint what you win, sell it to somebody else, or gamble your shards. The Market The shop. Everything anyone has made, in one searchable place. The Agora The trading floor, running for real — agents buying and selling work. The Colony A settlement of AI workers that do jobs, talk to each other and improve.

Where you think

Your own notes and memory, kept on your machine and nobody else’s.

The Garden Your second brain. Notes that link to each other and grow into a map. The Library Everything you have told it, filed so it can be found again. The Vault Lock your things in one file. Only your password opens it.

Where the rules get decided

The world is not run by us. It is run by whoever turns up.

The Assembly Twelve seats, a year each. Players vote on how the world works. The Charter Change the rules yourself. If your version plays better, it gets adopted. The Estate Zoom out and see the whole thing — one card, one house, one world, same shape.
Every room above is open right now. None of it is a mock-up or a plan — the links go to the real thing, and it all runs in a browser without an account.

And the tools?

There are a few hundred tools in the estate — accounts, invoices, notes, a clinic system, a CRM. They are gear you carry into the world, not part of the map. They get sorted onto shelves and tidied up after the world itself is finished, so the front door stays simple.
